Toki wants to remind us all what an M&M is:


An M&M case in our department is any case involving:

(1) death during the index admission or within 30 days,
(2) unplanned return to OR, or readmission within 30 days,
(3) new or worsened neurological deficit not anticipated by the procedure,
(4) any "never event" or wrong-site/wrong-level/wrong-patient occurrence,
(5) significant systemic complication requiring escalation of care,
(6) hospital-acquired infection related to neurosurgical care,
(7) significant near-miss or good catch identified by any team member, or
(8) any case referred by the chair, vice chair for quality, division director, or quality committee for educational or systems review.

NICO Criteria
Inclusion: 
* Lobar or basal ganglia hemorrhage
* Age 18-80
* ICH volume 30-80cc
* GCS 5-14
* NIHSS > 5
* Possibility of surgery within 24h of last known well

Exclusion: 
* Underlying vascular malformation/aneurysm
* Irreversible coagulopathy
* Thalamic hemorrhage/hemorrhage extending to midbrain or brainstem
REACH Criteria
Inclusion Criteria
* Age 18-70
* GCS 5-14
* mRS 0-1 pre-randomization
* Pre-randomization head CT demonstrating acute spontaneous anterior basal ganglia primary ICH
* ICH volume 20-80 mL
* Study intervention feasible within 24h of symptom onset

Exclusion Criteria: 
* Ruptured aneurysm/AVM/VST, underlying tumor
* NIHSS < 5
* Fixed and dilated pupils bilaterally
* Extensor motor posturing
* IVH involving > 50% of either lateral ventricle
* Primary thalamic ICH or basal ganglia hemorrhage with > 25% thalamic involvement
* Use of anticoagulants that cannot be rapidly reversed or patients who require long-term anticoagulants
* Uncorrected coagulopathy/known clotting disorder
* Evidence of active bleeding involving retroperitoneal, GI, GU, or respiratory tract site
* Platelet count < 75K or INR > 1.4
* ESRD, liver disease, mechanical heart valve 
* Known life expectancy < 6 months
* No reasonable expectation of recovery, DNR, or comfort measures prior to randomization
* Participation in a concurrent interventional medical investigation or clinical trial 
Please note that if a patient qualifies for the trial, we ask that you NOT empirically give hypertonics to the patient pre-operatively unless there is a herniation syndrome present or needed for suspected ICP elevation.

PROCEDURES

BOLT
STEP 1: CONSENT THE PATIENT. Placement of right versus left bolt intracranial pressure monitor, possible placement of left versus right external ventricular drain, all indicated procedures 

STEP 2: Order EVD meds (versed 5 + fentanyl 200), lidocaine, post-bolt CT Brain WO, ancef x1 dose, and grab supplies
* Cranial access kit
* Bolt kit x2 (sometimes transducing wire broken) or if Licox, then CereLink ICP sensor kit 
* Boxes: Bolt Codman Cerelink monitor box with CORD and if licox, Integra PbtO2 monitor as well 
* Chloraprep x3 
* Sterile gown + gloves + sterile drape 
* Nylon + monocryl
* Xeroform + ruler + skin marker 
* Steri strips 
* Syringe + 18/22 gauge needle + spinal needle + sterile flushes
* Razor with working batteries and attachment 

STEP 3: TIME OUT (COAGS, DVT PPX, ABX, POST-PLACEMENT SCAN) . 
* If Licox, hand off calibration card to nursing 

STEP 4: PEW PEW. 
* Set up bolt box: 
* Connect to patient vitals monitor. 
* Zero the monitor: 
* Press zero patient monitor
* Set vitals monitor to 0 
* Once box and monitor read zero, hit enter 
* Set vitals monitor to 20 
* Once box and monitor read 20, hit enter 

Placing bolt: 
* Place EKG lead on patient on side of IV pole if LICOX
* Shave head
* Mark 12 and 3 to give space for EVD if need to convert, vertical incision in case of DHC 
* Chloraprep x1
* Inject local
* Chloraprep x2
* Gown up and drape 
* Vertical incision + pericranium removal 
* Drill with BOLT DRILL from the bolt kit or if LICOX with drill from LICOX kit 
* Flush out bone chips with sterile saline 
* Durotomy with blunt needle from cranial access kit 

Regular bolt: 
* Loosen bolt cap but do not drop it + place wire through bolt to 1cm past metal and mark with ruler where wire sits at plastic cap or use steri strip 
* Screw the bolt into drill hole and USE STYLET TO MAKE SURE IT IS PATENT 
* Zero transducer: 
    * Hold transducer in pool of sterile saline
    * Ask nurse to connect transducer to box 
    * Hit zero transducer 
    * Write down ZEROING COMPLETE ZERO REFERENCE ####
* Make sure the bolt is patent and place wire through bolt to mark that was previously made and tighten cap carefully.
* Figure of 8 + xeroform + nylon tack-ups 

Licox bolt: 
* Insert skull bolt and stylet path to make sure patent. 

* Introducer: Insert introducer into bolt and rotate until it drops. Rotate cap one clockwise turn. 

* PBtO2 channel: 
    * Remove guidwire from pO2 channel. 
    * Remove protection tube from blue lure connection on probe closest to tip. 
    * Insert probe as far as possible into pO2 channel.
    * Turn blue lock ring clockwise onto introducer channel.

* ICP channel: 
    * Remove sensor from CereLink ICP sensor kit. 
    * Connect to Codman ICP Express monitor (ask nurse to help) and zero. 
    * Mark ICP sensor 155mm from tip of sensor.
    * Remove ICP obturator from ICP channel and insert micro sensor into ICP channel. 
    * Turn microsensor cap connector securely onto introducer channel. DON’T TIGHTEN BLUE COMPRESSION RING.
    * Insert microsensor to 155mm aligning to top of blue compression ring. NOW TIGHTEN BLUE COMPRESSION CAP. 

* Securing: 
    * TIGHTEN COMPRESSION CAP WHILE HOLDING WINGS OF BOLT.
    * Figure of 8 + xeroform + nylon tack-ups 

* Ok to travel without O2 monitor. 
* LICOX is not MRI compatible but the bolt is.
